Frequently Asked Questions

Does JPMAPORT connect to my brokerage?

No. JPMAPORT is a private investing journal. It is not a broker and not a trading platform. There is no account, no login, no order ticket, and no connection to any exchange or custodian. You enter your trades manually; JPMAPORT does the bookkeeping.

Are the prices and charts real?

All reference prices, candlestick data and charts in JPMAPORT are simulated and illustrative. They are generated locally on your device to help you visualise your journal — they are not real-time market data and are not investment advice.

Where is my data stored?

Everything you create — trades, notes, photos, watchlists, alerts and goals — is stored only on your device. Nothing is uploaded to any server. If you delete the app, the data is removed with it. You can also export the entire journal to a plain-text file at any time.

Can I move my journal to a new iPhone?

Yes, through the standard iOS device-to-device migration or by restoring from an iCloud or iTunes backup made on a previous device. Because data lives only on your device, the standard iOS restore flow is what handles it.

How do I attach a photo to a trade or note?

Open or create a trade or note, tap the attachment area, then choose Take Photo (Camera) or Choose from Library. JPMAPORT will request the corresponding system permission the first time. Photos stay on your device alongside the journal entry.

Does JPMAPORT support multiple currencies?

Yes. You can record trades in any currency. The app includes a built-in currency conversion calculator so you can quickly translate values between currencies using rates you provide.

Is JPMAPORT investment advice?

No. Nothing in JPMAPORT — including any chart, ratio, projection or example — is investment advice. JPMAPORT is a journaling and analysis tool only. Please consult a licensed professional for investment decisions.

I have a feature idea. How do I share it?

We love hearing from you. Email your idea to becipora79@gmail.com with the subject line "Feature Request". We read every suggestion.
