What is JPMAPORT?

JPMAPORT (also marketed as PortfolioX) is a private investing journal designed for individuals who manage global stock portfolios and want a disciplined, visual record of every decision they make.

JPMAPORT is not a broker and not a trading platform. There is no account, no login, no order ticket and no connection to any exchange or custodian. You enter your transactions; JPMAPORT does the bookkeeping so you can think clearly.

Key Features

Record Every Trade

Log buys and sells with shares, price, fees, currency, date and written rationale. Attach camera photos or library images directly to any trade or note.

Automatic Cost Basis

Average cost, position size, unrealised and realised results, allocation weight, closed positions and cash balance — all calculated locally.

Interactive Charts

Candlestick, line and mountain charts drawn entirely on device with no third-party library. Pinch, pan, and switch across seven time ranges.

Watchlists & Alerts

Build watchlists with notes, tags and personal reference levels. Mark in-app alerts on price, percentage or volume triggers.

Goals & Calculators

Define savings and accumulation goals with milestones. Use compound growth, position sizing, risk/reward and currency conversion calculators.

Offline News

Read bundled offline coverage, bookmark stories, and export your entire journal to a plain-text file at any time.

Private by Design

Everything stays on your device. No servers, no tracking, no analytics, no advertising. All reference prices are simulated and illustrative, and nothing in the app constitutes investment advice.

Tracking Notice

JPMAPORT does not track you. The app contains no advertising identifiers, no third-party analytics SDKs, no tracking pixels and no tracking domains. Read the full Privacy Policy for details.
