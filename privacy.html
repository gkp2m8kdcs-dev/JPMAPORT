Tracking Disclosure (App Store Tracking Statement)

JPMAPORT does not track you. The app contains no advertising identifiers (IDFA), no third-party analytics SDKs, no tracking pixels, no fingerprinting, no cross-app tracking and no tracking domains. JPMAPORT does not collect, transmit or share any personal data for tracking purposes, now or in the future. The "Allow tracking" prompt controlled by iOS App Tracking Transparency is not invoked because there is nothing to track.

1. Overview

JPMAPORT (also marketed as PortfolioX) is a private, on-device investing journal. This Privacy Policy explains what data JPMAPORT handles — which, in short, is almost none — and how the limited information the app touches is processed.

Your privacy is the product. There are no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party services that receive any of your data.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

All journal content you create is stored locally on your device only. This includes:

  • Transactions: buy/sell records, shares, prices, fees, currency, date, rationale.
  • Notes: written entries and attached photos.
  • Watchlists: instrument lists, tags, reference levels.
  • Alerts, goals and calculator inputs.
  • App settings: base currency, theme preferences, and similar local choices.

This data never leaves your device automatically. It is only exported if you explicitly use the in-app "Export Journal" feature to save a plain-text file.

3. Tracking Statement

For the purpose of App Store privacy disclosures:

  • NO Tracking — JPMAPORT does not track users across other apps or websites.
  • NO Advertising Identifiers — the IDFA is not accessed or used.
  • NO Tracking Domains — no network requests are made to tracking endpoints.
  • NO Cross-App Tracking — there is no SDK, pixel, or fingerprinting technique that follows you across apps.
  • NO App Tracking Transparency Prompt — the "Ask App Not To Track" prompt is not shown because JPMAPORT performs no tracking. The app behaves identically whether ATT is granted or denied.

This aligns with the value declared in the app's Privacy Manifest (NSPrivacyTracking = false, NSPrivacyTrackingDomains = []) submitted with the iOS build.

4. Data NOT Collected

JPMAPORT does not collect:

  • Contact info, identifiers, or user IDs.
  • Location data (precise or coarse).
  • Usage analytics, diagnostics, or performance data.
  • Purchase history or financial account information.
  • Browsing history, search history, or advertising data.
  • Health, fitness, or sensitive personal information.

5. Permissions Requested

JPMAPORT requests only the system permissions it needs to function, and only when you use the related feature:

  • Camera — to capture photos for your investment notes (e.g. attaching a screenshot of a chart or a paper receipt).
  • Photo Library (read) — to attach existing images to your notes.
  • Photo Library (write) — to save note images to your photo library when you choose to export them.

These permissions are granted or denied by you at the operating-system level. They are used solely for the feature you are interacting with and are not transmitted anywhere.

6. Reference Prices and Charts

All reference prices, candlestick data and charts displayed in JPMAPORT are simulated and illustrative. They are generated locally on your device for the purpose of visualising your journal. They are not real-time market data and are not investment advice.

7. Children's Privacy

JPMAPORT is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect any personal information from children. Because no data is collected at all, this requirement is satisfied by design.

8. Your Rights and Data Control

Because all journal data lives only on your device, you are in full control. You may at any time:

  • Edit or delete any trade, note, alert, watchlist item or goal inside the app.
  • Use the in-app "Export Journal" action to save a plain-text copy of your records.
  • Delete the app to erase all locally stored data.

9. International Users

No data leaves your device, so there is no cross-border data transfer to disclose. The app behaves identically in every region.

10. Changes to This Policy

If this Privacy Policy is updated, the revised version will be posted at this URL with a new "Effective" date at the top. Material changes will be highlighted inside the app on the next launch.
